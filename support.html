Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Zumus has no accounts and no cloud sync.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings, export a backup, and send the file to your new device. In Zumus on the new device, open Settings and restore from that file.

I deleted the app. Can my data be recovered?

Only from a backup file you exported, or from a device backup made before you deleted the app. Zumus keeps no copy anywhere else.

How do I send an invoice?

Open the invoice and share its PDF. You can send it by Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or save it to Files.
