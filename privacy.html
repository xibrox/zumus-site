Zumus Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Zumus is an app for tracking billable hours and issuing invoices. It is built to work entirely on your device.

We collect no data

Zumus has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. The app does not connect to the internet. Nothing you enter — clients, projects, time entries, invoices, your business details or your logo — is sent to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored in a database on your device. It stays there until you delete it or remove the app. Deleting the app deletes your data.

Files you choose to share

When you export an invoice PDF or a backup file, Zumus hands it to the system share sheet. Where it goes from there — Mail, Files, AirDrop, a cloud drive — is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Images you choose

If you add a logo, Zumus copies the image you pick into the app's own storage so it can be printed on your invoices. It does not access your photo library otherwise.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or another device backup, your operating system may include Zumus's data in that backup. This is handled by Apple or Google, not by Zumus.

Children

Zumus does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
